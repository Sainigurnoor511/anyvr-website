Terms of use

This website is an interactive preview of AnyVR, a virtual reality project by Gurnoor Singh Saini. It is provided as is, for information and personal exploration, without any warranty that it will be available, accurate or free of errors.

The photographs show the real places. The 3D places are artistic reconstructions and scans. They show scale and atmosphere, not survey-grade or archaeological detail. The guide preview uses prepared text and is not a live AI service.

The AnyVR name, logo, text and code belong to their creator. The 3D models and textures listed below belong to their authors and are used under the licenses shown. You may not extract them from this site and reuse them unless their own license allows it.

Privacy

This website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

If you join the early-access list, we store your email address, the place you picked (if any) and the date you joined. We use it only to email you when AnyVR opens, and we never sell or share it. To be removed at any time, ask through the project's GitHub page or reply to our email.

It stores one thing in your browser: whether you chose the light or dark theme. When you press Listen in the guide preview, that line of guide text, and nothing you type or say, is sent to Fish Audio to generate the voice; Fish Audio's free model may keep requests to improve its quality. If that service is unavailable, your browser's own speech voice is used instead. Links to GitHub, Sketchfab and Fish Audio take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.
