Questions

Do I need a VR headset for this website?

No. You can browse this website with a mouse, trackpad, keyboard or touch. The AnyVR app itself is built for VR headsets.

Are these real scans of the places?

The Valloire church and the Pyramid of Menkaure are photogrammetry scans; the Giza pyramids are all drawn from the Menkaure scan. The Eiffel Tower is a detailed model, and the Taj Mahal and its gardens are reconstructed for this preview. Each model's author is credited at the bottom of the page.

Is the guide a live AI assistant?

Not on this website. It shows sample guide styles and languages with prepared text, and it never records your voice.

Can I explore without the scroll animation?

Yes. Choose Reduce motion in the tour controls for a still introduction, then browse the destinations. Your device's reduced-motion setting is respected automatically.
