Questions

A few things
worth knowing.

Do I need a VR headset for this website?

No. This is an interactive 3D web preview. It works with a mouse, trackpad, keyboard, or touch. The AnyVR software project is built around immersive VR tours.

Are these exact scans of the monuments?

These are detailed procedural reconstructions created for this preview, not survey-grade scans. They communicate scale and atmosphere rather than archaeological precision.

Is the guide a live AI assistant?

The website demonstrates sample guide styles and languages with prepared text. It does not connect to a live AI service or record your voice.

Can I explore without the scroll animation?

Yes. Use Reduce motion in the tour controls for a still introduction and browse the destination images below. Your device's reduced-motion setting is respected automatically.
